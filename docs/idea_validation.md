# Idea Validation — MinutesMate AI

## 1. Target User & Context
- **Primary User:** Team leads, project managers, and administrative coordinators in small-to-medium teams who run weekly operational or project meetings.
- **Pain Point:** Converting raw meeting transcripts into structured minutes takes 30–60 minutes per meeting, and action items frequently slip through the cracks because owners or deadlines are vague.

## 2. The 7 Idea Validation Steps
1. **Problem Hypothesis:** Teams lose accountability after meetings because manual note-taking is slow and vague commitments ("someone should check this soon") go untracked.
2. **User & Market Evidence:** Workplace productivity studies consistently show professionals spend 3–5+ hours weekly in meetings and post-meeting documentation. Informal feedback from 5 working peers confirmed that writing minutes is tedious and follow-ups fail most often when deadlines or owners are omitted.
3. **Existing Alternatives:** Manual Google Docs/Word notes (slow, error-prone) or enterprise meeting bots like Otter/Fireflies (expensive, require bot permissions in live calls, and mostly produce long transcripts rather than accountability audits).
4. **Value Proposition:** Paste any transcript and immediately get structured minutes **plus** an automatic accountability audit that flags missing owners/deadlines and drafts ready-to-send follow-up nudges.
5. **Differentiating Angle:** Unlike generic transcript summarizers, MinutesMate AI acts as an **Action-Item Accountability Auditor**—calculating a Meeting Clarity Score, highlighting ambiguous tasks, and generating targeted follow-up messages for incomplete commitments.
6. **MVP Scope Validation:** A lightweight web app accepting pasted transcripts (with a 1-click sample transcript for instant testing) validates core value without requiring audio upload infrastructure or third-party calendar integrations.
7. **Success Criteria:** Users can generate accurate, structured minutes, spot missing owners/deadlines in under 15 seconds, and export clean Markdown notes with zero hallucinations.
